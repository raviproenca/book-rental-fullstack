# MedQuest Edu — Impeccable design context

This file captures **product and brand intent** for UI work. It is maintained by humans; do not infer audience or personality from the codebase alone.

## Design Context

### Users

- **Primary audience:** Medical students preparing for exams, often in **long, focused study sessions** with high cognitive load.
- **Typical context:** Switching between practice runs, review, and session configuration; mobile and desktop; possible use late at night or in bright environments—so **both light and dark themes must remain viable**.
- **Jobs to be done:** Answer and review questions efficiently, see progress and weak areas, configure practice (topics, counts, modes) without the interface adding friction or anxiety.

### Design principles

1. **Clarity under fatigue:** Hierarchy, contrast, and labels stay scannable after hours of study; microcopy explains outcomes, not brand slogans.
2. **Respectful density:** Students need information; avoid empty dashboards and mystery meat navigation—but **chunk** content so it does not overwhelm.
3. **Motion with purpose:** Animate for state changes, navigation, and feedback; respect **`prefers-reduced-motion`** and avoid gratuitous motion.
4. **Typography honesty:** The UI currently loads **Inter** and **JetBrains Mono** from Google Fonts (see [`src/index.css`](src/index.css)). When intentionally refreshing type, choose a **deliberate** display + body pairing aligned with the three brand words—not template defaults (see `.agents/skills/impeccable`).
